## Summary

This article describes the JadePuffer threat group's return with a new Go-based ransomware variant called **ENCFORGE**, targeting AI/ML infrastructure. The initial breach exploited **CVE-2025-3248** in the open-source Langflow framework and a 2021 Nacos authentication bypass vulnerability.

No standard technical indicators of compromise (IP addresses, domains, URLs, file hashes, or email addresses) were published in this article. The article is a descriptive news summary and does not include the specific IOCs used in the attack.
