# IOCs: The Odyssey Piracy Scams

**Source:** Malwarebytes Threat Intel - "The Odyssey piracy scams appear within hours of the movie's release"
**Date collected:** 2026-07-20

This article describes social engineering scams targeting users searching for pirated copies of Christopher Nolan's "The Odyssey" movie. Two main tactics were observed:

1. **Fake browser warnings** ("Browser Issue Detected") on cloned piracy sites with a "Fix It Now" button funneling users through malvertising networks.
2. **A fake movie download** disguised as a video file but actually a Windows executable.

**Note:** No standard technical IOCs (IP addresses, domains, URLs, file hashes, or email addresses) were published in this article. The article focuses on describing the social engineering tactics rather than providing specific indicators. The only concrete artefact identified is the malicious filename below.
