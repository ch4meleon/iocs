# IOCs: Hotel Wi-Fi DNS Poisoning Campaign (ReliaQuest Threat Spotlight)

**Date collected:** 2026-07-24

## Campaign Summary
A widespread DNS poisoning campaign targeting public Wi-Fi gateways at hotels, conference centers, and other shared venues. Attacker-controlled infrastructure used to steal Microsoft 365 corporate login credentials from traveling employees. Activity ongoing since at least June 2026. Tradecraft overlaps with APT28 / Fancy Bear / Forest Blizzard ("FrostArmada" campaign).
