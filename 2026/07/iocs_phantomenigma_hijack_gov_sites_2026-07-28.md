## Analysis

This article is a news summary about the PhantomEnigma malware operation that abuses compromised Brazilian government (.gov.br) portals to distribute malware. The article was provided by ANY.RUN and does **not** contain specific technical IOCs (IP addresses, domains, file hashes, email addresses) in its visible text content.

The article references:
- At least 20 compromised Brazilian government portals (municipal and police .gov.br sites)
- A Delphi-compiled installer named `Procuracao_Digital.exe`
- A malicious `index.js` script used in a patched Electron application
- Lure documents themed as "Ofício" (official summons) PDFs and "Procuração Digital" (digital power of attorney)
- Banking targets including Banco do Brasil
- The "Ofício-PC" QR-code campaign
