# IOCs: Europol flags 4,340 'horrific' URLs linked to The Com

**Source:** https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/07/24/europol-flags-4340-horrific-urls-linked-to-the-com/5278556

## Overview

The main article reports that Europol flagged 4,340 URLs for removal as part of Project Compass, targeting The Com network. The specific 4,340 URLs are not published in the article. However, related The Register articles about The Com's subgroups (Scattered Spider, ShinyHunters) contain domain IOCs associated with their phishing and exfiltration infrastructure, listed below.
