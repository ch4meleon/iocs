## Summary

Campaign ongoing since at least June 2026. Attackers compromise hotel/conference Wi-Fi captive portal gateways and use DNS poisoning to redirect business travelers to fake Microsoft 365 login pages. Activity observed across multiple US cities, India, and Saudi Arabia, targeting organizations in finance, legal, healthcare, energy, retail, and professional services.
