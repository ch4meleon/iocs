**Source:** Proofpoint Threat Insight blogs (July 2026)

## Notes

- The main article describes a new OWAReaper implant exploiting CVE-2026-42897 in Outlook Web Access. The actor-controlled domain used for C2 and exfiltration relay is `acocdn.com`.
- OWAReaper also uses three legitimate image CDN services as exfiltration proxies: `images.weserv.nl`, `i3.wp.com`, `slack-imgs.com`. These are not adversary-controlled but are relevant for detection.
- The ZimReaper indicators below (from the previous TA488 blog) are included for a complete picture of TA488 infrastructure.
