**Source:** https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/coldcard-users-lose-89m-bitcoin/

Context: Ongoing attack against Coinkite Coldcard hardware wallets exploiting a predictable RNG fallback (firmware regression from March 2021). Galaxy Research tracked waves 1-3 (~1,367 BTC from 4,385 addresses); a 4th wave was flagged by Alex Thorn (Galaxy) on 2026-08-02/03. The lists below are the researcher-published on-chain indicators. NOTE: The primary indicators for this incident are Bitcoin addresses and transaction IDs (not network IOCs); they are listed under the extra `bitcoin_address` / `sha256` (txid) sections below.
